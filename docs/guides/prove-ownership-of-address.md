---
title: How to prove ownership of an address
nav_title: Prove ownership of address
category: howto
---

A signature made with an address is itself the proof that you control that address. There is no separate "ownership proof" tool — message signing does the job.

Work through [Sign / verify a message](sign-verify-message), and when you reach the **Address** field, put in the address whose ownership you want to demonstrate.

Hand the resulting signature to the other party. Anyone can verify it against the same address without learning anything about your keys.
