---
title: How to freeze/thaw outputs
nav_title: Freeze/thaw outputs
category: howto
---

A frozen output is held back from every transaction the wallet builds. Thawing reverses this and returns the output to the spendable pool. The two actions are a pair; nothing is destroyed either way.

**Steps**

1. Open the **Coins** tab. If it isn't visible, switch it on under **View → Coins**.
2. Highlight the outputs you want to act on.
3. Right-click the selection and pick **Freeze output** or **Thaw output**.

Freezing is useful when you want to keep a particular output out of circulation — for example one you would rather not link to a future spend.
