---
title: How to switch to a different subaddress account
nav_title: Switch subaddress account
category: howto
---

Reach the account list through **Wallet → Account**, or by clicking the Account Switcher icon in the statusbar.

**What you can do here**

- **Switch accounts** — click any entry in the list.
- **Add an account** — click **New account**.
- **Rename an account** — double-click its **Label** cell, or right-click the row and choose **Edit label**.

Accounts cannot be hidden or deleted once created. Keep that in mind before adding accounts you only need briefly.
